A contract is between named parties. The buyer is the organisation that takes the obligation to pay, not the procurement mailbox or the team that prepared the papers. The supplier is the organisation that is awarded the work. If the note says the contractor will be named later, or that the authority will be confirmed on a letter that is not attached, that party is absent. Do not fill the gap with a guessed name.
