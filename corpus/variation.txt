A variation changes the scope, the price, or the time after the contract exists. Until a variation is agreed, the total amount is the sum already stated. An illustrative catalogue price, a previous year's fee, or an optional price that was declined is not a variation and is not the contract sum. The quote for the total amount should be the sentence that states the agreed fee.
