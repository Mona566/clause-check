The contract date is the date the parties made the agreement. That is often the signature date or the date of the award note. A later service start date, a workshop that has not been fixed, or a delivery date is not the contract date. If both a signature date and a start date appear, the contract date is the earlier agreement date, and the quote for that field should be the sentence that states the signature or award.
