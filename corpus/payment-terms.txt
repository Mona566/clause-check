Source: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/prompt-payment-policy

Public-sector buyers are expected to pay valid, undisputed invoices promptly. A common contractual statement of that duty is that payment terms are 30 days from receipt of the invoice. The payment term is the number of days until the sum is due. It is not a reminder date, not the day interest starts, and not the delivery date of the goods. When a memo says payment terms are 30 days, that figure is the payment term even if another sentence mentions a different number of days for interest or for a chase.
