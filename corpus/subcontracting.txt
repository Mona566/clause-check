Subcontracting is when the supplier asks another firm to carry out part of the work. The subcontract does not replace the parties to the main contract. The buyer remains the organisation that awarded the work, and the supplier remains the organisation that accepted it. A subcontractor's name is not the supplier unless the memo says that firm was awarded the contract.
