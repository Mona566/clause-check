A liability cap limits how much one party may have to pay if something goes wrong. Liquidated damages for delay are a remedy, often a sum per week or a fixed sum, and they are not the contract price. A memo can state a total amount of £48,000 and damages of £5,000. The contract sum is £48,000. The damages figure must not be quoted as the total amount.
