Contract summary

Hest Bank Council and Lumen Field Services Ltd entered this contract on 1 November 2024. The total amount for the survey is £48,000. If the final pack is more than ten working days late, the supplier pays damages of £5,000. That damages figure is not part of the contract sum.

Payment terms are 30 days from receipt of an undisputed invoice. The £5,000 is a remedy, not a second fee.
