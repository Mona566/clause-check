File note: contract award

Northline Borough Council has awarded a short contract to Harbour Analytics Ltd for a review of its public-records index. The contract date is 4 June 2025. The total amount is £18,500 (eighteen thousand five hundred pounds). Payment terms are 30 days from a valid invoice.

Harbour Analytics Ltd will deliver a written index report and a list of gaps. The work uses documents the council already holds. No other fee was agreed. This note is the whole of the commercial summary.
